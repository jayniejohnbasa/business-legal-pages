Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of this demonstration customer-support service (“we,” “us,” or “our”) may handle information when you contact us through a business Facebook Page or related messaging channel.

Important: This is a general mockup policy prepared from a sample workflow. Before using it for a live application, replace or confirm the details so they accurately describe the responsible legal entity, actual data practices, service providers, retention periods, and contact information.

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on what you choose to send, the service may process your message content and information made available through the messaging platform, such as your display name, account or user identifier, and conversation details. If you provide them, messages may also contain contact details, delivery addresses, order details, files, or other information relevant to your inquiry.

2. How information may be used

Information may be used to receive and respond to inquiries; provide information about products or services; understand and route customer-support requests; identify complaints or questions requiring a human response; maintain and improve customer-support operations; and protect the service against misuse.

3. Automated responses and human support

An automated assistant may use the message you send and business reference information (a knowledge base) to draft a response. The assistant is intended to answer from that reference information and may route unanswered questions or complaints to a human representative. Automated responses may be incomplete or mistaken; you may ask for a human representative.

4. Service providers and sharing

The workflow described for this demonstration may involve Meta/Facebook Messenger for communications, n8n for workflow automation, an AI service provider to generate responses, Google Docs or Google services for business reference material, and Telegram to notify an operator when human attention is needed. Information may be processed by these providers to the extent necessary to operate the workflow. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply.

We do not describe or promise any particular storage, deletion, encryption, or retention configuration in this mockup. The live operator must verify each provider's role and settings and disclose any additional recipients or integrations before launch.

5. Retention and security

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated support and operational purposes, or as required by applicable law. Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be used. No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You should avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or sensitive personal information through ordinary chat unless specifically required through a secure and appropriate process. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other action concerning your personal information. Requests should be directed to the business operating the page using its published contact details.

7. Children

This demonstration service is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. A live operator should establish and publish any age-related requirements that apply to its services.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised “Last updated” date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the business through the contact details published on the relevant Facebook Page or business website. Before launch, replace this instruction with a monitored privacy contact email or other designated contact channel.

This page is a demonstration template, not legal advice. The operator must review and adapt it to its actual practices and applicable privacy laws before publishing it for a live service.
